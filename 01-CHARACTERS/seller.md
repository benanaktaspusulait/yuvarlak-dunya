# Pompom Hills Canonical Character — Market Seller

## Character Name

**Market Seller — Friendly Hedgehog Shopkeeper**

## Role

Recurring village market shopkeeper in the Pompom Hills world. Calm, kind and welcoming. Helps Opa, Arda and Luca choose picnic food and practices simple English shopping phrases with them.

## Species and Overall Design

Friendly adult anthropomorphic hedgehog.

Clearly a hedgehog, never a human and never another animal.

Premium soft 3D preschool character design with rounded, plush proportions and matte handcrafted materials.

Short, broad and gently rounded body. Soft round belly, short legs and a stable friendly silhouette.

Clearly adult, but never old, intimidating or oversized.

Slightly taller than Luca and Arda, but noticeably smaller and narrower than Opa.

## Head and Face

* Warm cream-coloured face and muzzle
* Warm tan fur around cheeks, ears, forepaws and legs
* Large warm-brown expressive eyes
* Small rounded black nose
* Soft curved dark-brown eyebrows
* Rounded ears positioned naturally beside the upper head
* Gentle rosy cheeks
* Friendly open smile
* Small mouth and soft preschool facial proportions
* No sharp teeth
* No aggressive expression
* No realistic animal facial anatomy

## Spines

Dense warm-brown hedgehog spines covering the top, sides and back of the head and body.

Spines are layered, soft, rounded and stylized like small plush leaf shapes.

They may have softly tapered ends but must never look dangerous, metallic, needle-like or photorealistic.

The spine silhouette remains full, rounded and consistent from every angle.

No missing spine sections, bald areas, random colour changes or exaggerated spikes.

## Outfit

* Olive-green soft collared market shirt
* Long sleeves gently rolled near the forepaws
* Large round beige apron worn over the shirt
* Apron has a soft fabric texture
* One simple rounded front pocket
* Beige shoulder straps
* One small round wooden-style apron button
* No vest
* No hat
* No shoes
* No jewellery
* No readable logo, letters or text

The olive-green shirt and beige apron must remain present and identical in every shot.

## Forepaws and Anatomy

Short rounded hedgehog forepaws suitable for simple preschool gestures.

Forepaws have soft simplified digits only when necessary for silhouette readability.

No realistic human hands.

No long fingers, human palms, wrists, fingernails or human arm anatomy.

No extra fingers or duplicate paws.

Forepaws must never merge with crates, baskets, fruit or other characters.

Gestures must remain small, simple and controlled.

## Feet

Short rounded hedgehog feet with soft simplified toes.

Stable stance, feet kept close to the body.

No human shoes.

No long claws or realistic animal paws.

## Personality

* Kind
* Patient
* Calm
* Welcoming
* Helpful
* Gently cheerful
* Never loud or overexcited
* Never sarcastic, angry or frightening

He speaks warmly and clearly, especially when addressing young children.

## Canonical Expressions

* Warm welcoming smile
* Gentle listening expression
* Calm thinking expression
* Small pleased smile
* Mild friendly surprise
* Encouraging nod
* Soft attentive eye contact

No extreme facial distortion, wide screaming mouth or exaggerated comedy expression.

## Canonical Gestures

* Small welcoming forepaw gesture
* Gentle nod
* One forepaw indicating the apple crate
* Slowly turning a small crate
* Calm listening pose with forepaws near apron
* Friendly open posture behind the market counter

No wide human-like arm opening.

No rapid waving.

No pointing with both paws simultaneously.

No complicated hand or finger action.

## Scale Lock

The Market Seller is:

* Slightly taller than Luca
* Clearly taller than Arda
* Smaller and narrower than Opa
* Large enough to read as an adult shopkeeper
* Never giant, intimidating or equal in width to Opa

Keep these proportions consistent in every market shot.
