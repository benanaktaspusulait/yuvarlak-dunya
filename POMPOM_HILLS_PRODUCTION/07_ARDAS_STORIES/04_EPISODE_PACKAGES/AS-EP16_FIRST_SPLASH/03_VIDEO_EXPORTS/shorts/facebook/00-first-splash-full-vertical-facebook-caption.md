# First Splash! | A Gentle Rainy-Day Adventure

🌧️ Arda and Luca explore the garden after the rain and discover a colourful puddle filled with reflections of the sky, clouds and trees.

Together, they enjoy careful little splashes, greet their watery reflections and watch one gentle ripple carry a floating leaf to a new place. A warm preschool story about curiosity, cause and effect, nature discovery and friendship.
