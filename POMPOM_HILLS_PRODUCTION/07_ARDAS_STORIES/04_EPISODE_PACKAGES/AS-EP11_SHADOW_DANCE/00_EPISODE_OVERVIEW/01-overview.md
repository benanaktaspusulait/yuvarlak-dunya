## Logline

Arda tests waves, stops, spins, and jumps to discover that a shadow mirrors every movement — then Noah arrives and the discovery becomes a shared game.

---

## Story Promise

This is a complete, independently publishable Arda's Stories episode. Its hook, action, learning
payoff, and closing are contained within eight 15-second shots.

Arda makes the shadow discovery alone (Shots 01–04). Noah arrives at Shot 05 by walking into the
frame from camera-right. From Shot 05 onward the episode becomes a two-character shared game.
The rhyme (Shot 07) and walk-off (Shot 08) are delivered together.

## Learning Outcome

Children can follow one clear physical idea from setup through payoff in a calm, safe, repeatable
two-minute story. The solo-to-shared structure shows that a personal discovery becomes even more
joyful when shared with a friend.
