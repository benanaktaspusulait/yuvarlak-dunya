**0.0–1.3s** Arda takes his first step onto the path and says, **"Come on, shadow!"**
**1.3–4.0s** he moves into a gentle preschool jog — never sprinting; his single attached ground shadow matches each stride.
**4.0–5.2s** still jogging, he says, **"Run with me!"**
**5.2–8.3s** he takes four small controlled jogging steps with natural arm swings; no stumble.
**8.3–9.8s** he glances briefly down and says, **"Step for step!"** then looks ahead.

**12.8–15.0s** Arda plants both feet and comes to a full stop. Arms rest at sides. He smiles. Shadow at rest. The shot completes here — Arda is fully still.

Locked child-eye 32mm wide shot; constant framing, constant distance, no cut, zoom, orbit or sudden acceleration. The camera does not follow Arda along the path — it stays locked in place.
