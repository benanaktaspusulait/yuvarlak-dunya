# Shot 01 — A Wave

## Production Mode
FRESH QUALITY-RESET SHOT — Chain 1 Anchor

## Clean Start State
Arda stands in warm morning sunlight in the garden. Full body visible. One physically correct ground shadow. Garden layout, fence, shrubs and path entrance clearly placed. Camera at child-eye level, 32mm wide framing. Relaxed starting pose, weight centred.

## Complete Main Action
He raises his right hand and gives one small wave; shadow mirrors it. One larger wave follows at 8.6s.

## Completed End State
Arda's right hand fully lowered to his side. Looking down at his shadow, curious and settled. Both feet on the ground. Shadow at rest.

## Stable Final Anchor
Arda standing still, hand lowered, looking at shadow. No motion in progress.

Continue from `@image1`. Preserve Arda, clothing, garden, sun direction, scale and one ground shadow 1:1. From frame one Arda shifts his weight and notices the shadow — no frozen opening. 0.4s: **"Hello, little shadow!"** He raises his right hand and gives one small wave; the shadow mirrors it immediately. 5.5s: **"You waved too!"** He lowers his hand, tilts his head and makes one small side step; shadow copies instantly. 8.6s: **"Can you copy me?"** He gives one larger right-hand wave and a shoulder bounce; shadow stays synchronized. 13.8s: Arda lowers his hand fully, plants both feet and settles looking down at the shadow. No motion in progress. Shot completes here. Locked child-eye 32mm wide shot — no cut, zoom or angle change. Preserve the soft warm matte preschool baseline; no darkening, local-contrast growth, oversharpening, HDR, gloss or harsh shadows. Colour master: approved Shot 01 still — do not drift. Garden ambience only. No captions, text, music, extra characters or props.
