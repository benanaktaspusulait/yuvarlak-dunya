# Thumbnail OpenArt Prompt — Brave Little Days Combined (EP04, EP06, EP12)

This file follows the **Hero View OpenArt Prompt Standard** (9 sections) as defined in `POMPOM_HILLS_PRODUCTION/00_GLOBAL_RULES/SHOT_CONTINUITY_WORKFLOW.md` and the **Thumbnail Template** in `07-BRANDING/THUMBNAIL_TEMPLATE.md`.

## 1. Opening Statement
This is a premium master thumbnail image for Pompom Hills, designed to capture the cozy and gentle essence of bedtime and kindness routines, serving as the high-quality visual hook for a preschool compilation.

## 2. Format and Composition
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (1280x720)
- **Camera Height:** Child-eye level (~0.80m)
- **Lens Feeling:** 35mm
- **Composition Type:** Close-up character portrait (Hero Shot)
- **Depth Layers:** 
  - Foreground: Character face (filling 40% of the frame, positioned slightly to the left)
  - Middle ground: A glowing Pompom-star nightlight held by the character
  - Background: Soft, blurry, warm pastel bedroom environment
- **Readability:** Clean safe areas (left and right bottom corners kept clear for YouTube UI).

## 3. Primary World Identity
- **Core Identity:** Kiko's Home Bedroom during a soft evening transition.
- **Key Landmark:** A soft, glowing, round Pompom-star that emits a warm, comforting light.
- **Feeling:** A safe haven where the dark is cozy and every touch is gentle.

## 4. Environment
- **Ground Cover:** Soft, plush lavender carpet (out of focus).
- **Vegetation:** None (indoor).
- **Spatial Qualities:** Cozy, enclosed, comforting, and airy.
- **Sky/Horizon:** A soft indigo night sky visible through a small, round window with a friendly crescent moon.

## 5. Colour Direction
- **Palette:** Pompom Hills base palette (Pastels).
- **Dominant Colours:** Soft indigo, lavender, warm amber (from the star), and coral pink (Kiko's shirt).
- **Avoid:** Neon colors, pitch black, harsh contrast, oversaturated blues.

## 6. Style
- **Visual Language:** Pompom Hills v2.1 style (round, soft, toy-like).
- **Quality:** High-quality 3D preschool animation render.
- **Material Feel:** Matte, soft-touch, handcrafted felt and plush textures.
- **Lighting:** Warm, even lighting on the character's face; no harsh shadows; soft glow from the Pompom-star.
- **Technical:** No HDR, no gloss, no lens flare.

## 7. Mood
- **Mood Descriptors:** Calm, Safe, Cozy, Kind, Sleepy-happy.
- **Child Evocation:** "I want to hug this character and go to sleep feeling safe."

## 8. Important Differentiation
- **NOT LIKE:** The Bright Sun Meadow (too bright), The Stormy Forest (too scary), or The Busy Town (too crowded).
- **NOT A:** Generic movie poster, flat 2D illustration, or complex landscape.

## 9. Forbidden
- **NO:** Text, labels, signs, or logos in the image (metadata handled separately).
- **NO:** Multiple characters (Kiko only).
- **NO:** Buildings or complex structures.
- **NO:** Rendering issues like HDR, plastic-gloss, or sharp edges.
- **NO:** Sad or scared expressions (must be happy/curious).
