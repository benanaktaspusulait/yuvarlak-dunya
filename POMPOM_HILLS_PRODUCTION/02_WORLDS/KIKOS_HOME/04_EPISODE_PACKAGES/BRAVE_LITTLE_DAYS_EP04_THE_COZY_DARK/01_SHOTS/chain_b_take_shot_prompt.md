## EP04 — THE COZY DARK

**Core Message:** "The dark is safe when it's cozy."

**Emotional Beats:**
- **Shot 01-02:** Fear/Vulnerability. Kiko expresses fear; Opa offers presence.
- **Shot 03-04:** Magical Shift. Opa reframes the dark; the star light transforms the room.
- **Shot 05-06:** Discovery & Safety. The moon is a friend; the dark is "world resting."
- **Shot 07-08:** Resolution. "The dark can be cozy." Peaceful sleep.

**Dialogue Consistency:**
- Kiko Voice: Soft, slightly high-pitched, toddler-like.
- Opa Voice: Deep, warm, comforting, slow pacing.
- Repeated Keyword: "Cozy", "Soft", "Brave".
