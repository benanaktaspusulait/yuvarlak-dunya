## YouTube Description
Is the dark a little bit scary? Join Kiko and Opa in this gentle bedtime story.

In this warm Pompom Hills preschool animation, Kiko wakes up when her nightlight goes out. With Opa’s gentle reassurance and a look at the friendly moon, she discovers that the dark isn't scary at all—it can actually be very cozy!

This episode supports social-emotional learning by helping children feel safe and calm during their bedtime routine, turning fear of the dark into a sense of comfort.
