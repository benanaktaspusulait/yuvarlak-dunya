# Final Video Metadata

## Episode

Brave Little Days EP06 — Cozy Down, Sleepy Time

## Characters

Kiko and Mimi

## Recurring Prop

One small sky-blue felt Pompom Ball

## Final Story

Kiko wants to play one more game with her blue Pompom Ball. Mimi gently helps her notice that her hands are slowing down, her eyes are blinking slowly and a yawn has surprised her. Kiko lets the ball rest with her, moves to her Sleeping Corner with Mimi, gets beneath the blanket and follows Mimi's soft breathing guidance. As her body settles and her eyes grow heavy, Kiko calmly accepts that she is getting sleepy. Kiko and Mimi say goodnight and finish the story resting peacefully together.

## Lesson

Children can notice gentle sleepy clues in their bodies, slow down without hurrying and move from playtime towards bedtime through a calm routine, a cosy blanket and soft breathing.
