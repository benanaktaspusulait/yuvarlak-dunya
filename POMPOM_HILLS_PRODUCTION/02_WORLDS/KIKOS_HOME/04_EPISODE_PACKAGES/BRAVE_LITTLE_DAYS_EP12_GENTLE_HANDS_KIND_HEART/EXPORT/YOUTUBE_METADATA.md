## YouTube Description
Learn the magic of being gentle! Join Kiko in the garden as she discovers how to interact with nature's smallest friends with kindness and care.

In this sweet Pompom Hills episode, Kiko meets a beautiful butterfly. With Opa's guidance, she learns that having "gentle hands" allows us to be close to nature without hurting it. A perfect story for teaching toddlers and preschoolers about empathy, kindness, and respecting living things.

This episode supports social-emotional learning (SEL) by focusing on self-control, empathy, and gentle physical interaction.
