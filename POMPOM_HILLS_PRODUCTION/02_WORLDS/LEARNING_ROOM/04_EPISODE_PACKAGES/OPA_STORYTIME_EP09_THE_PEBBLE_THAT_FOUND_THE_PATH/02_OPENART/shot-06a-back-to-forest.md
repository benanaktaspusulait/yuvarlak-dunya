# OPENART TRANSITION PROMPT — SHOT 06A

## Purpose

Transition from the Reading Room back into the imagination forest for the hill challenge.

Duration: 8.0 seconds
Aspect Ratio: 16:9

## Reference Images

@image1: Approved final frame of Shot 06 — Reading Room, Opa + Luca + Noah around the open book, children thinking about "Up?"

@image2: Approved forest hill-base frame — exact first frame of Shot 07, sole authority for end-state forest composition including pebble at gentle rise base, rounded roots, pink petal upslope

## Reference Logic

Start EXACTLY from @image1.
End EXACTLY on @image2.

@image1 defines:
- Opa, Luca, Noah positions
- open-book state
- Reading Room layout
- camera framing

@image2 defines:
- forest floor composition
- gentle hill base
- pebble position
- rounded roots
- pink petal position upslope
- lighting
- imagination-world framing

The transition must feel like the audience is moving from the picture book back into the story world.

## Transition Performance

0.0–1.0:
Exact @image1 final-frame state.

Opa, Luca and Noah remain in their established positions.
All three continue looking into the already-open book.
No dialogue.
No mouth movement.
No character repositioning.

Camera begins one smooth controlled push toward the open book.

1.0–2.2:
The open book gradually fills more of the frame.
The illustration becomes clearer:
the same forest path,
the gentle hill beginning to rise,
rounded roots along the sides,
the same pink petal farther upslope.

The Learning Room remains visible only around the edges.

2.2–3.3:
As the page fills the screen, perform a soft visual match transition from the book illustration into the full imagination forest.

The room disappears only because the camera/page image fills the screen.

Do NOT teleport characters.
Do NOT blend Opa/Luca/Noah into the forest.
No portal.
No sparkles.

3.3–4.0:
Finish EXACTLY on @image2.

Forest composition, pebble position, roots, petal, lighting, scale and framing must match @image2 exactly.

Very slight natural forest motion only.
Do not move the pebble or petal significantly.

End on the exact state required for Shot 07 to begin seamlessly.

## Camera

One continuous controlled push-in only.
No cuts.
No sudden zoom.
No camera shake.
No reframe jump.

## Sound

0.0–1.8: soft indoor room tone, subtle page ambience
1.8–3.3: indoor ambience gently fades while soft forest leaves and light breeze fade in
3.3–4.0: forest ambience only

No music.
No whoosh.
No magical transition sound.

## Critical Locks

@image1 = exact starting frame.
@image2 = exact ending frame.
Do not redesign either image.
No new objects.
No character disappearance while still visible in the room.
No character morphing.
No pebble or petal spawning.
The pebble and petal appear only as part of the book illustration during the push-in, then resolve naturally into the exact @image2 forest state.

## Negative Prompt

abrupt cut, instant forest switch, half room half forest, portal, sparkles, vortex, character morphing, Opa in forest, Luca in forest, Noah in forest, book distortion, page warping, pebble spawn, petal spawn, abrupt zoom, camera shake, music, cinematic transition effect
