## OpenArt Video Prompt

SPOKEN DIALOGUE IS OPTIONAL AND MINIMAL.
No music.

Premium stylised 3D children's storytelling.
Warm Learning Room at the start.
Soft dreamy imagination forest at the end.
Preserve the soft matte Pompom Hills baseline.
No HDR, no gloss, no harsh shadows, no oversharpening.

4-second performance:

0.0–1.0:
Exact @image1 final-frame state.

Opa, Luca and Noah remain in their established positions around the open book.
All three continue looking down at the page.
No character repositioning.
No exaggerated motion.
No mouth movement unless a short optional Opa line is used.

Camera begins one smooth controlled push toward the open book.

1.0–2.2:
The open book gradually fills more of the frame.
The illustration becomes clearer:
soft forest floor,
warm leaves,
small rounded pebble,
single warm-pink petal,
same story-state as @image2.

The Learning Room remains visible only around the edges.

2.2–3.3:
As the page fills the screen, perform a soft visual match transition from the book illustration into the full imagination forest.

No portal.
No sparkles.
No magical vortex.
No character blending into the forest.
Opa, Luca and Noah do not appear inside the forest.

3.3–4.0:
Finish EXACTLY on @image2.

Hold the forest state only briefly.
Tiny natural leaf movement only.
Pebble and petal remain essentially in place.
This exact end-state must connect directly into Shot 05B.

## Camera

No camera shake.

0.0–1.8: soft indoor room tone, subtle page ambience

3.3–4.0: forest ambience only

abrupt cut, instant forest switch, half room half forest, portal, sparkles, vortex, character morphing, Opa in forest, Luca in forest, Noah in forest, book distortion, page warping, pebble spawn, petal spawn, abrupt zoom, camera shake, music, cinematic transition effect
