## Transition Performance

0.0–0.8:
Start EXACTLY from @image1.

Forest remains full-screen.
Pebble and petal stay essentially still.
Tiny leaf and grass movement only.

No dialogue.

0.8–2.0:
The existing forest composition gradually takes on the subtle visual quality of a printed/painted storybook illustration.

Do not change the object layout.

Pebble, roots, path and petal remain in exactly the same spatial relationship.

No magical glow.
No sparkles.
No portal.

2.0–3.2:
Camera begins one smooth controlled pull-back.

The edges of the physical open book become visible around the same forest illustration.

As the camera continues pulling back, Opa, Luca and Noah gradually enter the frame naturally because the book becomes smaller within the established Reading Room composition.

Do not spawn the characters.
They are simply revealed by the camera pull-back.

3.2–4.0:
Finish EXACTLY on @image2.

Opa holds the open book.
Luca sits screen-left.
Noah sits screen-right.

All three are looking at the updated hill-base illustration.

No mouths moving.
No dialogue.

Hold only briefly so Shot 06 begins immediately.
