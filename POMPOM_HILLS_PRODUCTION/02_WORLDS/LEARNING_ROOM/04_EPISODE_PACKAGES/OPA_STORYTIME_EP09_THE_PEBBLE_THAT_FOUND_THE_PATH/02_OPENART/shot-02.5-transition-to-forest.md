# OPENART TRANSITION PROMPT — SHOT 02.5

## Purpose

Transition from the Reading Corner into the imagination forest.
Duration: 4.0 seconds
Aspect Ratio: 16:9

## Reference Images

@image1: Shot 02 approved final frame — Learning Room, Opa + Luca + Noah around the open book.
@image2: Shot 03 approved first frame — forest floor, pebble + single pink petal.

## Transition Prompt

0.0–1.0:
Start EXACTLY from @image1.

Opa, Luca and Noah remain in their established positions.
All three continue looking into the already-open book.
No dialogue.
No mouth movement.
No character repositioning.

Camera begins one slow controlled push toward the open book.

1.0–2.2:
Camera moves closer.

The physical book gradually fills more of the frame.
The visible illustration inside the book becomes easier to read:
soft moss,
warm fallen leaves,
small rounded pebble,
single warm-pink petal.

Learning Room remains visible around the edges.

No sudden zoom.
No magical glow.
No character morphing.

2.2–3.3:
The open page now fills almost the entire frame.

Perform one soft visual match transition from the illustrated forest page into the full imagination forest.

The room disappears only because the camera/page image fills the screen.

Do NOT:
teleport characters,
blend Opa/Luca/Noah into the forest,
create a portal,
use sparkles,
use a fantasy vortex.

3.3–4.0:
Finish EXACTLY on @image2.

Forest composition, pebble position, petal position, lighting, scale and framing must match @image2 exactly.

Very slight natural forest motion only:
tiny leaf movement,
soft breeze.

Do not move the pebble or petal significantly.

End on the exact state required for Shot 03 to begin seamlessly.

## Camera

One continuous controlled forward push toward the book.
No cuts.
No sudden reframe.
Transition completes only when the book page fills the image.

## Sound

0.0–2.0: Soft indoor room tone + tiny book/page handling ambience.
2.0–3.3: Indoor ambience gradually fades while soft forest leaves/breeze fades in.
3.3–4.0: Forest ambience only.

No music.
No whoosh.
No magical transition sound.

## Critical Locks

@image1 = exact starting frame.
@image2 = exact ending frame.
Do not redesign either image.
No new objects.
No character disappearance while they are still visible in the room.
No character morphing.
No pebble or petal spawning.
The pebble and petal appear only as part of the book illustration during the push-in, then resolve naturally into the exact @image2 forest state.

## Negative Prompt

abrupt cut, instant forest switch, character morphing, Opa in forest, Luca in forest, Noah in forest, half room half forest, distorted book, book morphing, extra characters, extra pebble, extra petal, glowing portal, magical portal, sparkles, fantasy vortex, fast zoom, camera shake, sudden reframe, text, captions, music, transition whoosh
