## Action and Dialogue

**0.0–1.5s:** Opa turns toward the camera with a bright, excited expression. His eyes are wide behind glasses. He leans slightly forward and says with playful energy: **"Ready? Let's count!"** One small wing gesture outward. Opa's tone is inviting — like starting a game.

**1.5–3.5s:** Opa turns slightly left and points one wing toward the butterfly. His expression shifts to gentle discovery. He says warmly: **"One butterfly!"** He nods once. His eyes follow his own wing gesture.

**3.5–5.5s:** Opa's wing gestures once toward both flowers together. He says with growing excitement: **"Two flowers!"** Small excited head tilt.

**5.5–7.5s:** Opa looks up toward the three birds on the branch and makes one gentle presenting gesture toward the group. He says clearly: **"Three birds!"**

**7.5–9.5s:** Opa's wing gestures toward the four leaves on the branch. He says clearly: **"Four leaves!"** His expression is focused, encouraging. Small nod.

**9.5–11.5s:** Opa looks down toward his feet where the five ladybugs sit. His eyes widen with playful surprise. He crouches slightly (squat, his characteristic low perch) and says with delight: **"Five ladybugs!"** He looks genuinely impressed.

**11.5–14.0s:** Opa straightens up, turns toward the camera and says warmly: **"Great counting!"** He gives one small proud nod. Glasses remain perfectly stable and correctly positioned. He holds a warm smile for the final 1 second. Natural breathing. Eyes alive.
