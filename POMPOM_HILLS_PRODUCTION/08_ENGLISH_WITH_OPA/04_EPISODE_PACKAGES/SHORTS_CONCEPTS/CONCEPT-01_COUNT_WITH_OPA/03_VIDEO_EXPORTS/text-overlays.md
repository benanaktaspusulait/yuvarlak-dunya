# Count 1–5 with Opa — On-Screen Text Overlays

## Purpose
Optional text overlays for post-production. These appear on screen during the Reel to reinforce the counting visually. They are NOT generated by OpenArt — they are added in video editing after the14-second clip is produced.

## Design Rules
- Font: rounded, preschool-friendly, bold but soft
- Colour: white (#FFFFFF) with warm soft shadow for readability
- Size: large enough to read on phone screen, but not covering Opa or countable objects
- Position: upper-left or upper-right corner, away from the countable objects
- Animation: gentle fade-in (0.3s), hold, gentle fade-out (0.3s)
- No bouncy, flashy or distracting animation

## Text Overlay Schedule

| Time | Text | Position | Notes |
|------|------|----------|-------|

## Notes
- Text overlays are optional — the Reel works without them (Opa's voice carries the counting)
- If adding overlays, keep them consistent across the entire Reel
- Do NOT add animated objects (butterfly, flowers, birds, leaves, ladybugs) — they are already in the video
- Only the number/word overlay is added in post-production
