# What Colour Is It? — OpenArt Native Audio Dialogue Map

## Production Method

- OpenArt generates movement and dialogue together in all three shots.
- Auto Sound / Native Audio: **ON**
- Voice / Speech: **ON**
- Music: **OFF**
- Use the manually assigned approved saved Arda voice for Arda.
- Use the manually assigned approved saved Luca voice for Luca.
- One speaker at a time; no overlap; only active speaker moves his mouth.
- No separate voice track or silent-video pass.
