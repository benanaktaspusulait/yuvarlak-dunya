# Shot 01 — What Colour Is It? — Red + Yellow

## Purpose

Teach red and yellow with two relaxed question-answer cycles.

## Production

- Duration: 15 seconds
- Format: 9:16 vertical
- Location: Opa's Garden, warm sunny morning
- Characters: Arda and Luca
- Production Mode: `FRESH QUALITY-RESET SHOT`
- Clean Start State: approved original Shot 01 `@image1`; complete object inventory visible
- Complete Main Action: red and yellow question-answer cycles
- Completed End State: Arda has calmly redirected attention to the blue sky
- Stable Final Anchor: both boys settled; Arda's gaze/gesture rests toward the sky
- Next-Shot Dependency: `EXACT FRAME CONTINUITY REQUIRED`

## Scene Flow and Exact Dialogue

- 0.0–0.8: Arda gently presents the existing red apple.
- 0.8–2.6: Arda: **“What colour is the apple?”**
- 2.6–3.5: Luca takes time to look at the apple.
- 3.5–4.8: Luca: **“It's red!”**
- 4.8–6.5: relaxed shared reaction and breathing room.
- 6.5–7.3: Arda indicates the already-visible yellow sun.
- 7.3–9.1: Arda: **“What colour is the sun?”**
- 9.1–10.0: Luca takes time to look toward the sun.
- 10.0–11.3: Luca: **“It's yellow!”**
- 11.3–13.0: relaxed shared reaction and breathing room.
- 13.0–14.0: Arda slowly redirects attention to the already-visible blue sky.
- 14.0–15.0: pose and camera settle; no dialogue.
