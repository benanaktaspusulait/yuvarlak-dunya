## Visual Prompt

Duration: 10 seconds. 9:16 vertical.

Premium Pompom Hills 3D preschool animation. Rounded plush-clay shapes, soft pastel colours, matte handcrafted toy-set materials, warm gentle sunset light, medium-low contrast, soft shadows and calm bedtime atmosphere.

Opa sits safely and comfortably on a wide sturdy rounded tree branch in Pompom Hills. Opa is the canonical wise owl character, with his exact approved proportions, face, feather colours, large round glasses and soft handcrafted appearance. He faces slightly toward camera so his eyes and mouth remain clearly readable.

The background begins at warm late sunset / early twilight. The sky is peach, lavender and soft blue, never harsh orange and never dramatically dark. A pale moon is already visible in the sky at frame 0. A few extremely faint stars are already visible at frame 0 so no celestial object needs to pop into existence later.

Across this shot, twilight develops only very gently and continuously: the already-visible faint stars become just slightly easier to see, and the already-visible moon becomes only slightly brighter. No sudden day-to-night transition, no time-lapse jump, no lighting snap, no star pop-in and no moon teleportation.

Camera at calm child-eye / slightly upward storybook angle, fully locked. No pan, tilt, zoom, orbit, reframing or cinematic camera movement.

Opa remains securely seated on the same branch throughout. No jumping, flying, hopping or position reset. His movements are tiny and sleepy: gentle blinking, subtle breathing, small head turns and relaxed wing movements only.

Keep Opa and the environment softly lit and clearly readable throughout. Preserve the soft pastel preschool look. Do not increase contrast, saturation, sharpness, HDR, gloss or dramatic shadows. If the image changes at all, it may become slightly softer and calmer, never harsher.

## TIMING AND ACTION

### 0.0–1.0s — CALM VISUAL HOOK

Begin immediately on Opa already sitting peacefully on the branch at sunset.
He looks directly toward the viewer with a warm, sleepy expression.
One gentle natural blink.
No dialogue yet.
Subtle non-musical evening ambience continues.

### 1.0–4.2s

Opa gives one tiny friendly head tilt toward camera and says softly:

"Goodnight, little one."

Warm direct eye contact.
Natural bedtime speaking voice: very soft and intimate, but still clearly recognizable as the approved Opa voice.
Do not sing and do not make the line rhythmic.

### 4.2–5.0s

Opa relaxes his head back to neutral and gives one slow comfortable blink.
The faint stars become only slightly easier to see.
No abrupt lighting change.

### 5.0–9.0s

Opa gently lifts his gaze toward the sky without turning his whole body and says:

"The stars are watching."

During this line, the already-present faint stars continue to become gradually more visible.
Opa's body remains securely seated and stable.

### 9.0–10.0s

Opa's eyes softly return toward the viewer.
He settles into a calm friendly expression.
No dialogue.
Stable final frame for Shot 02 continuity.
