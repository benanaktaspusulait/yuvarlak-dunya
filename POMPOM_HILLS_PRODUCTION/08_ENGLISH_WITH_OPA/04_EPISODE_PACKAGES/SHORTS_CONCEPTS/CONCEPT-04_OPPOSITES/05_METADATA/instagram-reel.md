# CONCEPT-04 — Instagram Reel Caption

## Caption
⬆️⬇️ Big! Small! Up! Down! Fast! Slow!

Arda and Luca are learning OPPOSITES! 🤪

Can your child say them too?

#LearnEnglish #OppositesForKids #PreschoolEnglish #PompomHills #EnglishForToddlers #KidsEducation #VocabularyBuilding #ToddlerLearning #FunLearning

## Reel Notes
- Hook: Big rock vs tiny pebble — visual comedy
- Fast rhythm: new pair every ~2 seconds
- 2-shot structure: Shot 01 (15s) + Shot 02 (12s) = 27s total
- Interactive: child can repeat each word
- CTA: "Which pair is your child's favourite? ⬆️⬇️"
- No music, voices only
