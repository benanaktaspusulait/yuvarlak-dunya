## Critical Teddy Physicality Rule

One soft brown teddy bear has physically existed INSIDE the gift box from before the beginning of Shot 01.

It does NOT spawn. It does NOT materialize. It does NOT enter the scene. It does NOT descend from above. It does NOT grow from the box.

The teddy does not appear at a timed cue. It becomes visible only as a direct consequence of the existing lid physically uncovering the existing interior of the box.

**Teddy scale is fixed from the instant it first becomes visible. No growth from tiny to full size. No pop-in. No fade-in. No magical appearance.**

## Teddy Lock

Exactly ONE teddy bear.

Small preschool-safe brown teddy bear:
- Rounded head
- Two small round ears
- Soft brown plush body
- Small dark eyes
- Small friendly stitched-looking smile
- Short rounded arms
- No clothes, no accessories

The teddy is SMALL enough to fit naturally inside the gift box.

The teddy's visible head is proportionally small enough to emerge naturally from the existing box interior. Do not enlarge the box and do not enlarge the teddy to force the reveal.

When revealed, most of the teddy remains inside the box. It does NOT jump out. It does NOT animate. It does NOT wave. It does NOT speak.

## Action and Dialogue

**0.0–0.6s:** Continue exactly from @image1. Opa's rounded wing is already gently resting against the top edge of the closed lid beside the bow, in direct physical contact and ready to lift. The box remains 100% closed. No gap is visible. Opa looks at the box with excited anticipation.

**0.6–2.2s:** Without repositioning or making a second reach, Opa continues directly from the existing contact pose and gently lifts the same lid upward in one smooth motion. The bow remains attached to the lid. As the lid physically clears the box opening, the teddy already inside becomes visible. The teddy does not appear at a timed cue — it becomes visible only as a direct consequence of the existing lid physically uncovering the existing interior of the box. No sparkle. No magic effect. No scale growth. Teddy appears at its final fixed size from the first visible pixel.

**2.2–2.9s:** Opa sees the teddy. His eyes widen naturally behind his glasses. Small delighted reaction. No dialogue during this short reaction.

**2.9–5.5s:** Opa looks toward the teddy and says warmly and delightedly: **"A teddy bear! Hello!"** On "Hello!" Opa makes one small friendly rounded-wing wave beside his own body. The teddy stays completely still inside the box.

**5.5–6.5s:** Final warm hold. Opa smiling toward camera. The teddy remains clearly visible inside the open box. Natural breathing and eye movement. No further action.
