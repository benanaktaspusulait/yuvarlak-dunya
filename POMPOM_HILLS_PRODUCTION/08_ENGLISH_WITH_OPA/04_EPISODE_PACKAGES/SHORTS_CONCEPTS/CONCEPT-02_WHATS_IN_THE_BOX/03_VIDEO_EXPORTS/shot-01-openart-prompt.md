## Action and Dialogue

**0.0–0.8s:** Opa notices the closed gift box and gives a small curious head tilt. Natural breathing and eye movement only.

**0.8–3.0s:** Opa looks at the box and says clearly: **"What's in the box?"** He makes one small rounded-wing presenting gesture toward the box.

**3.0–3.7s:** Opa glances briefly toward the camera with a playful curious expression, inviting the child to wonder with him. No speech. Under 1 second.

**3.7–6.0s:** Opa looks back at the box and says excitedly: **"Let's open it!"** He begins one slow rounded-wing reach toward the lid.

**6.0–7.0s:** Opa's wing completes the reach and arrives beside the lid in a clear, single, unambiguous ready-to-open position. The wing is angled toward the lid edge, positioned so that one simple upward motion will lift it in Shot 02.

**7.0–7.5s:** Continuity hold. Opa beside closed box, wing positioned to open. Box is STILL COMPLETELY CLOSED — lid is 100% sealed, no crack, no gap. Opa looks excited. Final anchor pose.
