Opa opens the box lid. Teddy bear, already physically inside, becomes visible. Opa reacts with delight: "A teddy bear! Hello!" Teddy stays inside box. Warm payoff.

## Critical Teddy Physicality Rule

One soft brown teddy bear has physically existed INSIDE the gift box from before the beginning of Shot 01. It does NOT spawn. It does NOT materialize. It does NOT enter the scene. It does NOT descend from above. It does NOT grow from the box.

The teddy does not appear at a timed cue. It becomes visible only as a direct consequence of the existing lid physically uncovering the existing interior of the box.

**Teddy scale is fixed from the instant it first becomes visible. No growth from tiny to full size.**

## Teddy Lock

Exactly ONE teddy bear.

Small preschool-safe brown teddy bear:
- Rounded head
- Two small round ears
- Soft brown plush body
- Small dark eyes
- Small friendly stitched-looking smile
- Short rounded arms
- No clothes, no accessories

The teddy is SMALL enough to fit naturally inside the gift box.

The teddy's visible head is proportionally small enough to emerge naturally from the existing box interior. Do not enlarge the box and do not enlarge the teddy to force the reveal.

When revealed, most of the teddy remains inside the box. It does NOT jump out. It does NOT animate. It does NOT wave. It does NOT speak.

## Box Mechanics Lock

The same one gift box from @image1 remains on the table.

Opa opens the box using one simple physical motion.

The lid:
- Stays one rigid object
- Remains attached visually to the bow
- Lifts upward only once
- Does not duplicate
- Does not deform
- Does not vanish
- Does not fly away

The box body stays completely stationary on the table.

Opa does NOT pick up the entire box.

## Exact Dialogue
1. Opa: "A teddy bear! Hello!"
