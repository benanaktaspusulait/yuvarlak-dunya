## Purpose
Opa discovers a closed gift box, asks "What's in the box?", invites the child to wonder, reaches toward the lid. Box stays closed. Teddy stays invisible. Sets up Shot 02 reveal.

## Final Frame Lock — CRITICAL

At 7.5s the shot settles into its final anchor pose:

- Opa is beside the closed box
- **One rounded wing is positioned directly beside the lid edge, angled clearly toward the lid in a single unambiguous ready-to-open pose**
- The wing is NOT resting casually — it is positioned so that Shot 02 can begin with one simple upward lifting motion
- Box remains fully closed — lid is %100 sealed, no crack, no gap
- Teddy is completely invisible inside the box
- All furniture and environment elements remain unchanged from @image1
- Camera composition remains identical

This approved final frame becomes @image1 for Shot 02. The cleaner this pose, the smoother the lid-opening motion in Shot 02.
