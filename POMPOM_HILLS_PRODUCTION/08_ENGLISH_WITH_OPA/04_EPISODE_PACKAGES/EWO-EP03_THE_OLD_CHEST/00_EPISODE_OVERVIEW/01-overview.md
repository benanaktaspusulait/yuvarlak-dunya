## Logline
Opa finds a forgotten old chest on his platform. Inside are five mysterious objects — and the last one is a key. But what does it open? Nobody knows. Until Arda finds the answer.

## Story Arc

The episode has a real mystery structure:
- Objects discovered one by one (shots 01–06)
- Each object feels complete but also raises a question
- The large key (shot 02) becomes the through-line: "What does it open?"
- Arda finds a small hidden latch on the platform (shot 07) — the key fits
- Inside: the music box (shot 08)
- All objects find their homes (shots 09–10)

This transforms EP03 from "object show-and-tell" into a genuine mystery with a satisfying payoff.
