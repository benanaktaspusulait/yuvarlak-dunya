## 2. Episode Context
The group has arrived at the wooden bridge after their forest walk. As they step onto the bridge, a sudden gust of wind catches the picnic cloth draped over Opa's basket and lifts it onto a low-hanging branch. This is a Fresh Take Shot — action-heavy, setting up the cloth rescue that drives the episode.

## 15. Take Shot
**FRESH TAKE SHOT**

This is a Fresh Take Shot, meaning creative freedom is granted for the action sequence. The cloth flying from the basket to the branch is the dramatic centerpiece. Wind effects, character reactions, and physical comedy are prioritized over dialogue.

**Fresh Take Guidelines:**
- Cloth must fly naturally — caught by wind, lifted from basket, carried to branch
- Landing on branch must look believable — cloth drapes over branch
- Characters react with genuine surprise and alarm
- Opa's basket remains secure — only the cloth moves
- The moment should feel sudden and unexpected
- Comedic timing — the cloth lands perfectly on the branch like it was placed there
