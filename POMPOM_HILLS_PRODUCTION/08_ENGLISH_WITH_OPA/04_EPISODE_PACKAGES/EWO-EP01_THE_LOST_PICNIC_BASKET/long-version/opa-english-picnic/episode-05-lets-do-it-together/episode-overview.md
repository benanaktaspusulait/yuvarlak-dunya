## Episode Summary
The group arrives at the bridge. A sudden gust of wind lifts the picnic cloth from the basket and carries it onto a low branch. Arda can't reach it. Luca offers to help. Opa steadies the branch with his rounded wing while the boys work together to free the cloth. They succeed, and as they cross the bridge, the beautiful lake comes into view — their picnic destination.

## Emotional Arc
Arrival → Alarm (cloth blown) → Frustration (can't reach) → Teamwork → Triumph → Wonder (lake reveal)

- **Phrases**: "I can't reach it", "I can help", "Let's do it together", "Well done", "We made it"
