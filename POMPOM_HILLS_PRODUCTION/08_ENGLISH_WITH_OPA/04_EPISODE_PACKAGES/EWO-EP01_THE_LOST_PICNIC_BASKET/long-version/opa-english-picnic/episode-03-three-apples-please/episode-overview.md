# Episode Overview — EWO-EP03: Three Apples, Please

## Series Context
**Series**: Pompom Hills — English with Opa
**Episode**: EWO-EP03 — Three Apples, Please
**Episode Position**: Episode 3 of 6 (The Lost Picnic Basket arc)
**Theme**: Visiting the village market; ordering food; filling the basket; polite requests

## Story Summary
Opa, Arda and Luca arrive at the village market to fill their empty picnic basket. They visit a friendly hedgehog seller's fruit-and-bread stall. Opa asks what they need. Arda eagerly points at the apples. The seller asks how many. Arda orders three apples politely. The seller places them in the basket. Luca then orders bread and a small cake. The seller adds those too — silently, without an extra spoken line. Opa thanks the seller. Arda notices the basket is full. The group sets off toward the forest path.

## Characters Present
- **Opa** — large round light-green owl; guide; asks what they need; thanks the seller; announces forest departure
- **Arda** — boy 2-3y; orders three apples; excited about full basket

- **Market Seller** — friendly adult hedgehog shopkeeper; brown-cream fur, beige apron, olive vest, soft rounded spines; asks "How many?"; places apples, bread and cake box into basket

## Location
Village market square — wooden stalls with colourful awnings, flower arrangements, warm daylight. The fruit-and-bread stall is the main focus. Red apple crates visible. Bread on display. Small closed cake box on counter. Forest path exit visible nearby in the background.
