7. **Forest path exit visible in background** throughout all three shots — it is the destination.
8. **Market Seller is the hedgehog** — not a human, not a different animal. Same character in all three shots.
9. **Arda is a boy** — he/him/his only, never she/her.
10. **Opa has no hands/fingers** — rounded wings only for all interactions.
