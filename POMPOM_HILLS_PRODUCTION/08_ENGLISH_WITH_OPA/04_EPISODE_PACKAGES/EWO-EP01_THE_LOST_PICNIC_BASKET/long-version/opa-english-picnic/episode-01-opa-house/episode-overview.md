# Episode Overview — EWO-EP01: Opa's House

## Series Context
**Series**: Pompom Hills — English with Opa
**Episode**: EWO-EP01 — Opa's House
**Episode Position**: Episode 1 of series
**Theme**: Arrival and greeting; introducing the picnic plan; discovering the basket is missing

## Story Summary
Arda and Luca arrive at Opa's tree house. They greet Opa excitedly and confirm they are ready for their picnic. Opa asks where the basket is. Luca guesses it is behind the green chair — but it is not there. Arda spots a ribbon near the steps. Opa praises Arda's observation and says they should follow the ribbon to find the basket.
