# Episode Overview — EWO-EP02: Where Is the Basket?

## Series Context
**Series**: Pompom Hills — English with Opa
**Episode**: EWO-EP02 — Where Is the Basket?
**Episode Position**: Episode 2 of series
**Theme**: Following the ribbon clue; finding the basket; discovering it is empty; deciding to go to the market

## Story Summary
Following the ribbon trail from Episode 01, Opa, Arda, and Luca descend from the platform to the tree base. Opa asks where the basket is. Arda suggests looking under the stone steps. Luca spots the basket near the trunk — a folded red-white cloth is tied to its handle. Arda celebrates. Opa praises Luca, then notices the basket is empty. Arda suggests going to the market. Opa agrees, and the trio sets off — Opa carrying the basket, Arda holding the cloth, Luca pointing toward the village.
