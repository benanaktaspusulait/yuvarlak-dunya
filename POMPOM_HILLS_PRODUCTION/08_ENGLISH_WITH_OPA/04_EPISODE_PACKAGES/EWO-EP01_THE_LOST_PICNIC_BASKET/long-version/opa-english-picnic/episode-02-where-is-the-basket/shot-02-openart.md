## 1. Title
**EWO-EP02-S02: The Basket Is Found**

## 2. Episode Context
Episode 2 of Pompom Hills — English with Opa. Following Arda's suggestion to look under the steps in Shot 01, Luca suddenly spots the basket near the tree trunk. The basket has a folded red-white cloth tied to its handle. Arda celebrates the find. Opa praises Luca, then notices the basket is empty. Arda suggests going to the market. Opa agrees, and the trio prepares to leave — Opa carrying the basket, Arda holding the cloth, Luca pointing toward the village.

## 3. Shot Purpose
Deliver the basket discovery, reveal the empty-basket twist, establish the cloth as a separate prop, and set up the market trip. This is the resolution of the basket-search arc and the bridge to the next episode.
