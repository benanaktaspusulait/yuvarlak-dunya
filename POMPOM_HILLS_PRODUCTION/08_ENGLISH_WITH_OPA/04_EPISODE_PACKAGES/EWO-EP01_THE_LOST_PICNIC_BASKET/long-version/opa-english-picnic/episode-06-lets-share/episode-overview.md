# Episode Overview — EWO-EP06: Let's Share

## Series Context
**Series**: Pompom Hills — English with Opa
**Episode**: EWO-EP06 — Let's Share
**Episode Position**: Episode 6 of 6 (season finale)
**Theme**: Sharing, gratitude, friendship, enjoying a meal together

## Story Summary
Arda, Luca, and Opa arrive at the lakeside picnic spot after recovering the cloth at the bridge (Ep05). They spread the cloth, set down the basket, and admire the view. When they unpack the basket, they discover the cake box contains only one piece. Luca offers to share — half for Arda, half for him. Opa observes that sharing makes everything sweeter. Arda thanks Luca. The episode ends with a warm, peaceful picnic moment by the lake.
