## 1. Title
**EWO-EP06-S02: Only One Piece**

## 2. Episode Context
Episode 6 of Pompom Hills — English with Opa. Continuing from Shot 01 where the cloth was spread and the basket set down. Arda and Luca now unpack the basket — apples and bread come out first. Then the cake box is opened for the FIRST TIME in the entire series. Inside: only one piece of cake. Luca offers to share. This is the emotional turning point of the episode.

## 3. Shot Purpose
Unpack the basket contents (apples, bread), open the cake box for the first time, reveal the single piece of cake, and deliver the sharing offer. Establishes the problem (only one piece) and the solution (sharing) in one continuous action.
