## Key Continuity Rules
1. **Basket lid**: CLOSED in Shot 01, OPENED in Shot 02, stays open in Shot 03
2. **Cake box**: CLOSED until Shot 02 opening, stays open in Shot 03
3. **Cake piece**: ONE piece only, visible in Shot 02, broken in half in Shot 03
4. **No knife**: Cake is soft, breakable by hand — Luca breaks it, NOT Opa
5. **Opa's role**: Observer and wisdom-giver; does NOT touch cake, basket, or food
6. **Arda's pronouns**: he/him/his only — NEVER she/her
7. **One speaker at a time**: No overlapping dialogue in any shot
8. **First line timing**: Each shot's first line by 1.5s (max 2.0s)
9. **Duration**: Each shot exactly 15 seconds
10. **Aspect ratio**: Each shot exactly 9:16 vertical
