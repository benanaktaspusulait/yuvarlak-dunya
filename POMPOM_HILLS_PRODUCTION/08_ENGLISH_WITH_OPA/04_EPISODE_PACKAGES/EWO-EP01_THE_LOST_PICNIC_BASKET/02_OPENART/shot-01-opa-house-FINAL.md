## Story Context

Arda and Luca arrive at Opa's open-air tree home for a picnic adventure. Opa greets them warmly from the platform, but the picnic basket is missing from its usual place. Luca checks behind the reading chair — nothing. Arda spots a small red-and-white ribbon near the platform steps. The group decides to follow it as a clue. English emerges naturally from the excitement of arrival and the small mystery.
