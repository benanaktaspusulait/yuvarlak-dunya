## Story Context

The group follows the small red-and-white ribbon clue down from Opa's platform to the base of the tree. Among the wildflowers near the tree roots, Luca discovers the hidden wicker basket — and with it, a folded red-and-white checkered picnic cloth tied loosely to the basket handle from outside. The basket contains no food at all. The group decides to head to the village market to fill it. This shot establishes both the basket and the picnic cloth for the rest of the episode.
