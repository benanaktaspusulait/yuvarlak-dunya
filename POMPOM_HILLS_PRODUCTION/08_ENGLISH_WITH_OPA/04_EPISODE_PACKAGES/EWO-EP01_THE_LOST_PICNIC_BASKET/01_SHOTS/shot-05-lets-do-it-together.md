## Purpose

## Story Summary
Opa, Arda and Luca arrive at the small wooden bridge. A gust of wind blows the picnic cloth off the top of the basket and onto a low branch just above head height. Arda reaches up but cannot get it alone. Luca says he can help. Opa says "Let's do it together." The children reach up together and retrieve the cloth. Opa says "Well done!" The lake becomes visible beyond the bridge.
