# Shot 03 — Three Apples, Please

## Production Mode
LINKED CONTINUITY SHOT — from Shot 02

## Clean Start State
`@image1` = mandatory approved final frame of Shot 02. Opa, Arda and Luca are standing at the base of the tree home, facing down the colourful stepping-stone path toward the village. The empty picnic basket is in Opa's wing.

## Duration
Exactly 15 seconds

## Aspect Ratio
9:16 vertical

## Learning Focus
**Main pattern: "Three apples, please."**
Target vocabulary: apples, bread, three, please, how many, here you are

## Purpose
Introduce the village market as a new vivid location. Arda and Luca practise asking for items and quantities naturally through a quick, real transaction with the friendly market seller. The shot ends with the basket looking full and the group ready to continue.

## Story Summary
Opa leads Arda and Luca along the stepping-stone path to the colourful village market. At the fruit and bread stall, Opa asks what they need. Arda points at apples and asks for three. The seller hands them over. Luca adds bread. The basket is placed on the stall counter, the items go inside, and the group leaves with a full basket, heading toward the forest path.

## Canonical Character Lock

### Opa
- Large round light-green owl. Not a human. No human hands or fingers.
- Huge warm-brown eyes, round golden-yellow glasses always on.
- Warm orange beak and rounded orange feet.
- Cream face and belly feathers, layered soft-green body feathers.
- Thick knitted warm-brown scarf always worn.
- Carries the wicker picnic basket.

### Arda
- Smaller toddler boy, visually 2–3 years old.
- Sky-blue T-shirt with chest badge, light-blue trousers, tan shoes.
- Dark-brown hair, rosy cheeks.

### Luca
- Slightly taller preschool boy, visually 3–4 years old. Modest height difference over Arda.
- Cream T-shirt with green sleeves and mountain emblem, khaki-green shorts, striped socks, orange trainers.

### Market Seller
- A friendly adult character — simple, warm and clearly a background supporting role.
- Cheerful expression, apron, natural market-stall appearance.
- Human proportions consistent with the soft Pompom Hills 3D animation style.
- No complex backstory or elaborate design needed.

## Canonical Location Lock
Scene takes place at a colourful village market square that is visually distinct from the tree home.

### Required Details
- Bright wooden market stalls with colourful awnings.
- A fruit and bread stall prominently in frame: crates of red apples, a bread basket, and other simple produce.
- Cheerful warm market atmosphere.
- Soft Pompom Hills colour palette — rounded, toy-like, no harsh realism.
- Warm daytime lighting consistent with Shot 01 and Shot 02.
- The stepping-stone path from Opa's tree home visible at the edge of the scene, connecting this new location to the previous one.

## Scene Flow
1. Shot opens with a quick wide shot of the colourful village market.
2. Opa, Arda and Luca walk toward the fruit and bread stall.
3. Opa places the empty basket on the stall counter.
4. Opa asks what they need for the picnic.
5. Arda points at the red apples excitedly.
6. Seller asks how many.
7. Arda says "Three apples, please."
8. Seller counts out three apples and hands them over — "Here you are."
9. Luca points at the bread and it is added to the basket.
10. Opa thanks the seller.
11. The basket is now visibly fuller — apples and bread inside.
12. Shot ends with the group picking up the basket and turning toward the forest path ahead.

## Dialogue
**Opa:** What do we need?

**Arda:** Apples!

**Seller:** How many?

**Arda:** Three apples, please.

**Seller:** Here you are!

**Luca:** And some bread too!

**Opa:** Thank you very much.

**Arda:** The basket looks full!

**Opa:** Through the forest path. Let's go!
