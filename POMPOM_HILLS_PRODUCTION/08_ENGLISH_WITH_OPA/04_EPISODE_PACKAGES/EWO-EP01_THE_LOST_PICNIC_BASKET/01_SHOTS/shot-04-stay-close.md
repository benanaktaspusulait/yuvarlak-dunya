# Shot 04 — Stay Close

## Production Mode
LINKED CONTINUITY SHOT — from Shot 03

## Clean Start State
`@image1` = mandatory approved final frame of Shot 03. Opa carrying the full basket, Arda and Luca beside him, all three facing toward the forest path entrance. The market is behind them and the forest path opens ahead.

## Duration
Exactly 15 seconds

## Aspect Ratio
9:16 vertical

## Learning Focus
**Main pattern: "Stay close."**
Target vocabulary: stay close, wait, I'm here, together

## Purpose
Introduce the magical forest path and create a brief, charming distraction moment. The shot teaches spatial and safety language naturally through Luca's butterfly encounter. The episode gains movement and a gentle beat of gentle guidance before the bridge scene.

## Story Summary
Opa, Arda and Luca walk along the magical forest path. A butterfly drifts across the path. Luca takes two steps toward it. Arda immediately notices and calls out. Opa calmly says to stay close. Luca turns back, says he is here, and rejoins the group. They continue walking together. At the end, the sound of water and the edge of a wooden bridge become visible ahead.

## Canonical Character Lock

### Opa
- Large round light-green owl. Not a human. No human hands or fingers.
- Golden glasses and brown scarf always on.
- Carrying the wicker picnic basket with one rounded wing.

### Arda
- Smaller toddler boy. Sky-blue T-shirt, light-blue trousers, tan shoes.

### Luca
- Slightly taller preschool boy. Cream T-shirt with green sleeves, khaki shorts, striped socks, orange trainers. Modest height difference over Arda.

## Canonical Location Lock
Scene takes place on a magical forest path — visually distinct from both the tree home and the market.

### Required Details
- Beautiful forest path with tall rounded trees and a soft green canopy.
- Warm sunlight filtering gently through the leaves.
- Wildflowers and small plants along the path edges.
- A single friendly-looking butterfly visible mid-shot.
- The path is clearly walkable and safe — no dark corners, no scary elements.
- A wooden bridge or the sound of water becomes just visible at the far end of the path in the final frames.
- Warm natural daytime lighting consistent with previous shots.

## Scene Flow
1. Shot opens with the group entering the forest path from the market-side direction.
2. Opa walks ahead with the basket; Arda and Luca follow.
3. A butterfly drifts across the path.
4. Luca watches it and takes two small steps sideways off the main path.
5. Arda turns and notices immediately.
6. Arda calls out — "Luca, wait!"
7. Opa turns calmly and says — "Stay close, little explorers."
8. Luca turns back toward the group — "I'm here!"
9. Opa extends a wing gently and gestures the group forward.
10. Arda says — "Let's walk together."
11. Luca rejoins and all three walk forward side by side.
12. Shot ends as the first glimpse of the wooden bridge and the sound of water appear ahead.
