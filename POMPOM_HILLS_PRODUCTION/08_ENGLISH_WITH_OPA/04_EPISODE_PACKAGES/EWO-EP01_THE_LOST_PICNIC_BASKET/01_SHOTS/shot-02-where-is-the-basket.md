# Shot 02 — Where Is the Basket?

## Production Mode

Luca is pointing toward a red-and-white ribbon near the platform steps. The tree home platform, hanging lanterns, arched bookshelves, tea table and green landscape remain exactly as in Shot 01.

## Duration
Exactly 15 seconds

## Aspect Ratio
9:16 vertical

## Learning Focus
**Main pattern: "Where is the basket?"**
Target vocabulary: under, behind, there, found

## Purpose
Follow the ribbon clue from Shot 01 and locate the empty basket near the base of the tree home. Opa and the children use spatial language naturally while searching. The shot ends when the basket is found — and discovered to be empty — which motivates the market trip.

## Story Summary
Opa, Arda and Luca follow the red-and-white ribbon clue down the steps and around the base of the tree. Arda checks under the steps. Luca spots the basket tucked among the flowers at the base of the trunk. Opa opens it — it is completely empty. Arda immediately says they need to go to the market.

- Wavy dark-brown hair, large warm-brown eyes, light freckles.
- Cream-white T-shirt with green sleeves and mountain emblem.

## Scene Flow
1. Shot opens exactly from Shot 01's final frame. All three move from the platform edge toward the steps.
2. Opa carefully descends the steps; children follow.
3. Arda immediately crouches and checks under the lowest step.
4. Luca looks behind a flower clump near the trunk.
5. Opa asks where the basket could be.
6. Luca points excitedly toward the base of the trunk — **"There it is!"**
7. Arda rushes over and crouches beside the basket.
8. Opa leans forward and opens the basket lid with one wing.
9. All three look inside. The basket is completely empty.
10. Opa says it is empty and they need food.
11. Arda turns, points toward the path and says they must go to the market.
12. Shot ends with all three looking down the colourful stepping-stone path toward the village direction.

## Dialogue
**Opa:** Where is the basket?

**Arda:** Is it under the steps?

**Luca:** No... wait — there it is!

**Arda:** I found it!

**Opa:** Well done, Luca.

**Opa (opening the basket):** Oh. The basket is empty.

**Arda:** Empty? Then let's go to the market!

**Opa:** Good idea. To the market!
