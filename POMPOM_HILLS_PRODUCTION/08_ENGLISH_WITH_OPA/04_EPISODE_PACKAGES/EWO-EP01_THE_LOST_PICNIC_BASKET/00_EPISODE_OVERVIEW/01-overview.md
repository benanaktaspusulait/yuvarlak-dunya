## Learning Outcome

Children absorb 6 natural English expressions through story and context:

1. "What can you see?"
2. "Where is the basket?"
3. "Three apples, please."
4. "Stay close."
5. "Let's do it together."
6. "Let's share it."

No direct instruction. No classroom. Language is embedded in action, problem-solving and relationship.
