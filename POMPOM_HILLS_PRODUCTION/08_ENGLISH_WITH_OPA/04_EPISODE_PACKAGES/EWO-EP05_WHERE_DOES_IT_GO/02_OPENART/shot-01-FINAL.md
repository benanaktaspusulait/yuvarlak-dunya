## Story Context
Opa's platform is gently out of order after a creative day — a teapot on the step, a book on the chair, a stump seat in the wrong spot. Instead of simply tidying, Opa picks up a small pen with a wing and draws a simple illustrated map of the platform showing where each thing belongs. This becomes a game.
