## Logline
Opa's home is gently out of order after a creative day. Instead of tidying alone, Opa draws a little map — and transforms putting everything back into the best game of the afternoon.

| Shot | Learning Pattern |

| 02 | "It goes on the table." / "It goes under the shelf." |
| 03 | "Is it behind the chair?" / "No, it's in front." |
| 04 | "Put it next to the teapot." / "Like this?" |
| 05 | "Is that right?" / "Yes, that's right!" |
| 06 | "Now everything is in the right place." / "It looks perfect!" |
