## Logline
Opa unrolls an old map of Pompom Hills with an X — a secret bench under a willow with a view of the whole valley. Following the directions teaches every location and direction word naturally along the way.

## Story Arc — A Real Journey

The episode is structured as a genuine adventure with mounting anticipation:
- Shot 01: Map revealed; first direction given
- Shot 02: First landmark — big stone; turn left
- Shot 03: Open meadow; near/far question
- Shot 04: Small discovery mid-walk — something beautiful along the way
- Shot 05: Around the hill; something spotted through leaves
- Shot 06: Map consultation — where exactly are we?
- Shot 07: Almost there — the willow is close
- Shot 08: Final approach — one more step
- Shot 09: Arrival — the secret bench is found
- Shot 10: Sitting together — the view — map folded with care
