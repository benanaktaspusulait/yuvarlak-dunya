# Facebook Short 02 - One-Foot Balance Challenge

## Task
Create a standalone Facebook Reel/Short from a fresh take. Do not use continuity from Short 01 or Short 03.

## Output
- Duration: 12.5 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical
- Resolution: 1080x1920
- FPS: 24
- Characters: Luca and Arda only
- Location: Butterfly Meadow
- First spoken line by 0.5-0.8 seconds
- No intro, logo animation, text, subtitles, or end card

## Fresh Take and First Frame
Use a new approved first frame. Luca stands on the left and Arda on the right. Both stand upright with both feet fully visible and grounded. Use a slightly wider vertical composition so the legs and feet remain readable. Luca is naturally a little taller; Arda is smaller but not miniature.

## Camera and Visual Style
Stable child-eye vertical demonstration framing, slightly wide for safe balance movement. Keep both full bodies and both feet inside frame. No camera shake, zoom, pan, orbit, cut, or scene change. Soft handcrafted Pompom Hills preschool 3D style, warm gentle Butterfly Meadow daylight, matte materials, medium-low contrast, restrained saturation.

## Action and Exact Dialogue

### 0.0-1.4 - Hook
Luca opens both arms for balance and looks directly toward the viewer.

LUCA: "Can you balance with us?"

### 1.4-3.0 - Demonstration
Luca gently lifts one foot only a small distance above the grass. No hopping.

LUCA: "One foot up!"

### 3.0-4.8 - Copy
Arda opens his arms and copies Luca with a small controlled foot lift.

ARDA: "Mine is up!"

### 4.8-6.5 - Adjustment
Both make tiny natural balance corrections. Do not freeze or hold a blank pose.

LUCA: "Stay steady!"

### 6.5-8.2 - Challenge Beat
Arda makes one small controlled wobble of the upper body without stepping, falling, hopping, or kicking.

ARDA: "I'm balancing!"

### 8.2-10.0 - Reset
Both gently lower the lifted foot and place both feet fully on the grass.

LUCA: "And feet down!"

### 10.0-12.5 - Viewer Challenge
Arda opens both hands toward the camera in a friendly invitation. End immediately after the line.

ARDA: "Can you do it too?"

## Safety
The lifted foot stays low, the hold stays brief, and both feet return to the ground before the final invitation. No falling, hopping, jumping, spinning, deep balance pose, or long one-foot hold.

## Audio
No music, soundtrack, melody, singing, humming, or rhythmic bed. Use only the exact dialogue, very soft Butterfly Meadow ambience, and subtle natural clothing/grass movement sounds. No dialogue overlap. Listener mouth stays closed.
