# Facebook Short 01 - Tiny Seed to Big Tree

## Task
Create a standalone Facebook Reel/Short from a fresh take. This is not a crop of the long episode and does not continue from another short.

## Output
- Duration: 12 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical
- Resolution: 1080x1920
- FPS: 24
- Characters: Luca and Arda only
- Location: Butterfly Meadow
- First spoken line by 0.5-0.8 seconds
- No intro, logo animation, text, subtitles, or end card

## Fresh Take and First Frame
Use a new approved first frame for this short. Luca stands on the left and Arda stands on the right. Both are fully visible from head to toe, standing in Butterfly Meadow. Luca is naturally a little taller; Arda is smaller but not miniature. Keep the floral arch, stepping-stone path, pastel flower beds, and soft rolling hills visible. Start moving immediately.

## Camera and Visual Style
Stable child-eye vertical demonstration framing. Keep both characters large, readable, and fully inside frame. Preserve natural headroom and visible feet. No camera shake, zoom, pan, orbit, cut, or scene change. Soft handcrafted Pompom Hills preschool 3D style, warm gentle daylight, matte materials, medium-low contrast, restrained saturation.

## Action and Exact Dialogue

### 0.0-1.5 - Hook
Luca immediately bends his knees into a small crouch and gestures down toward the ground.

LUCA: "Can you be a tiny seed?"

### 1.5-3.0 - Copy
Arda copies Luca and lowers into a clear tiny-seed crouch.

ARDA: "Tiny seed!"

### 3.0-5.0 - Grow
Luca begins rising smoothly, opening his arms as if growing upward.

LUCA: "Now grow, grow, grow!"

### 5.0-7.0 - Payoff
Both stand tall and reach their arms overhead.

ARDA: "I'm growing tall!"

### 7.0-9.5 - Branches
Both keep arms raised and open their fingers like tree branches. Their bodies remain active with a small natural stretch.

LUCA: "Reach your branches up!"

### 9.5-12.0 - Viewer Challenge
Arda turns warmly toward the camera and makes a small inviting gesture. End immediately after the line.

ARDA: "Can you grow too?"

## Audio
No music, soundtrack, melody, singing, humming, or rhythmic bed. Use only the exact dialogue, very soft Butterfly Meadow ambience, and subtle natural clothing/grass movement sounds. No dialogue overlap. Listener mouth stays closed.

## Negative Prompt
music, soundtrack, melody, singing, humming, narrator, voice-over, extra dialogue, subtitles, text, watermark, logo intro, extra characters, duplicate Luca, duplicate Arda, character swap, side swap, mirrored staging, wrong outfit, miniature Arda, enlarged Luca, scale drift, character redesign, camera shake, zoom, pan, orbit, internal cut, montage, frozen pose, long smile hold, dead air, empty opening, falling, jumping, hopping, spinning, dangerous movement, dark lighting, HDR, glossy plastic, harsh shadows, oversharpened, oversaturated, wrong meadow
