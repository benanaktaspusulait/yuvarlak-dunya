# Facebook Short 03 - Low, Middle, High

## Task
Create a standalone Facebook Reel/Short from a fresh take. This is an independent mini movement game, not an extracted segment and not a continuation of another short.

## Output
- Duration: 12 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical
- Resolution: 1080x1920
- FPS: 24
- Characters: Luca and Arda only
- Location: Butterfly Meadow
- First spoken line by 0.5-0.8 seconds
- No intro, logo animation, text, subtitles, or end card

## Fresh Take and First Frame
Use a new approved first frame. Luca stands on the left and Arda stands on the right. Both are upright, full-body, and fully visible. Luca is naturally a little taller; Arda is smaller but not miniature. Keep the Butterfly Meadow floral arch and path readable. The first movement begins immediately.

## Camera and Visual Style
Stable child-eye vertical demonstration framing. Keep both characters large and readable with feet visible during every level change. No camera shake, zoom, pan, orbit, cut, or scene change. Soft handcrafted Pompom Hills preschool 3D style, warm gentle daylight, matte materials, medium-low contrast, restrained saturation.

## Action and Exact Dialogue

### 0.0-1.5 - Low Hook
Luca immediately bends his knees and lowers into a clear, safe low position.

LUCA: "Can you go low?"

### 1.5-3.0 - Copy
Arda copies Luca and lowers into the same level.

ARDA: "Low!"

### 3.0-4.5 - Middle
Both rise to a clear half-height middle position, between crouching and standing.

LUCA: "Middle!"

### 4.5-6.2 - High
Both stand tall and raise their arms overhead.

ARDA: "High!"

### 6.2-8.8 - Recap
Luca quickly demonstrates the readable sequence low, middle, high. Each level gets a clear visible beat.

LUCA: "Low, middle, high!"

### 8.8-10.8 - Copy Recap
Arda repeats the sequence with Luca, staying energetic and readable.

ARDA: "I know all three!"

### 10.8-12.0 - Viewer Turn
Luca makes a small inviting gesture toward the camera. End immediately after the line.

LUCA: "Your turn!"

## Pacing
Every 1-1.5 seconds must include a new level, gesture, reaction, or clear body change. No purposeless silence longer than 0.7 seconds. Do not hold any pose for a long time.

## Audio
No music, soundtrack, melody, singing, humming, or rhythmic bed. Use only the exact dialogue, very soft Butterfly Meadow ambience, and subtle natural clothing/grass movement sounds. No dialogue overlap. Listener mouth stays closed.

## Negative Prompt
music, soundtrack, melody, singing, humming, narrator, voice-over, extra dialogue, subtitles, text, watermark, logo intro, extra characters, duplicate Luca, duplicate Arda, character swap, side swap, mirrored staging, wrong outfit, miniature Arda, enlarged Luca, scale drift, camera shake, zoom, pan, orbit, internal cut, montage, frozen pose, long smile hold, dead air, empty opening, unclear levels, slow inactive pacing, jumping, hopping, spinning, falling, dangerous crouch, dark lighting, HDR, glossy plastic, harsh shadows, oversharpened, oversaturated, wrong meadow
