## Description
Can you grow from a tiny seed into a strong tree? 🌱🌳

Join **Luca and Arda** in Butterfly Meadow for a playful preschool movement adventure!

In this PomPom Yoga episode, little learners can follow along as Luca and Arda:

🌱 Curl down like tiny seeds  
🌞 Grow up tall  
🙌 Reach high  
🌳 Sway like trees  
🦶 Practice simple balance  
↕️ Move low, middle, and high  
✨ Shake, freeze, and finish strong  

This gentle movement game helps preschoolers practice:

- body awareness
- balance and coordination
- listening and following instructions
- simple movement sequences
- left/right and level awareness
- confidence through active play

The big idea is simple:

**MOVE → STRETCH → BALANCE → HAVE FUN!**

No special equipment is needed — just a little space to move safely.
