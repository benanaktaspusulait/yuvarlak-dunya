## 15-second action + dialogue
**0.0-0.5** Hold exact `@image1`. Tiny blink / breathing only.

**0.5-3.0** Noah hears the creek and looks toward the nearby stones.

**NOAH:** "Listen! The creek is making a sound."

**3.0-6.0** He takes one or two small steps closer and crouches naturally. Hands remain away from stones.

**NOAH:** "I wonder what these stones sound like."

**6.0-9.5** Noah points to one smooth stone without touching it.

**NOAH:** "Maybe this one can make a sound too."

**9.5-12.0** He raises one HUMAN hand above the chosen stone. No contact.

**NOAH:** "I'll try it gently."

**12.0-15.0** Noah remains crouched with one hand hovering above the stone.

**NOAH:** "Ready?"
