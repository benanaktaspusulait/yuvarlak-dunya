## Story purpose
Noah makes one gentle leaf sound and identifies that his own action caused it.

## 15-second action and dialogue
**0.0–0.5**  
Hold exact `@image1`.

**0.5–2.2**  
Noah lowers the already-hovering HUMAN hand and gives the broad green leaf **ONE gentle tap only**.

**SFX:** one soft natural leaf "tap-rustle" contact.

**2.2–4.8**  
His hand lifts away again. He looks pleasantly surprised at the leaf.

**NOAH:**  
"Oh! I made that sound."

**NOAH:**  
"It was soft and rustly."

"Can I make a pattern?"

**11.5–15.0**

"I'll try two taps."
