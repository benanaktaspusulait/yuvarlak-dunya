# MRT-EP02 — Shot 01 — Listen to the Leaves

## Shot ID
MRT-EP02-S01

## Story purpose
Introduce Little Forest and Noah's curiosity. The learning idea is **listen first**: Noah notices that leaves can make interesting natural sounds.

## First-frame / Take Shot requirement
Create a fresh approved Shot 01 first frame in **Pompom Hills — Little Forest** before video generation.

- Keep open visual space around him for natural movement.
- Soft dimensional handcrafted preschool look; medium-low contrast and restrained saturation.

## 15-second action and dialogue
**0.0–0.5**  
Hold the exact approved `@image1` frame. Noah makes a tiny natural blink/breath only.

**0.5–3.5**  
Noah takes two small steps through the leaves. His shoes create a faint dry-leaf rustle. He looks down, curious.

**NOAH:**  
"Listen... the leaves sound crunchy!"

**3.5–6.5**  
He gently brushes one fallen leaf with the toe of his shoe. A soft natural rustle is heard. He smiles and crouches slightly, keeping both human hands visible.

**NOAH:**  
"What else can they do?"

**6.5–10.5**  
Noah reaches toward one broad fallen green leaf and pauses just before touching it. His attention is fully on the leaf.

**NOAH:**  
"I want to try a sound."

**10.5–15.0**  
He settles beside the leaf, hands relaxed and ready, but does **not** tap yet. He gives a small excited smile.

**NOAH:**  
"Ready, leaf?"

End in a stable pose that makes Shot 02 easy to continue: Noah beside the same broad green leaf, one human hand hovering nearby but not touching it.

## Sound

## Critical end-frame requirement
