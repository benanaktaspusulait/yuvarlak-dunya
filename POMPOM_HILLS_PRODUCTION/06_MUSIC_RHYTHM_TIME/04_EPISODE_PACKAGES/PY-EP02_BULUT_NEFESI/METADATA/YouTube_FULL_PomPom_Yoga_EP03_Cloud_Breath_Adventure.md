## Description
Can you move slowly like a cloud? ☁️

Join **Kiko and Noah** near Rainbow Bridge for a playful PomPom Yoga adventure filled with gentle breathing, simple movement, listening, counting, and imagination.

In this episode, little learners can follow along as Kiko and Noah:

☁️ Find a slow-moving cloud  
🙌 Open their arms gently  
⬇️ Float their hands down  
🔢 Count with a comfortable breath  
👐 Shake their hands and soften their shoulders  
☝️ Trace a cloud in the air  
💧 Listen to the river  
👧 Let Kiko take the lead  
👦 Let Noah follow  
👋 Finish with a cheerful recap and goodbye  

This episode is designed as a simple preschool movement game, not a long meditation.

Children can practice:
- body awareness
- listening and attention
- gentle movement
- following simple instructions
- turn-taking
- counting
- copying movements
- calm, comfortable breathing

No special equipment is needed — just a little safe space to move.
