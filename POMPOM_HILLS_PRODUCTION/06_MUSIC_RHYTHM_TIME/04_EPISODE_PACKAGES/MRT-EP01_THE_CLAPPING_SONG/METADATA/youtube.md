Can you clap the rhythm with Kiko, Noah and Mimi? 👏🌈

Noah discovers that he can make a sound with his hands. One clap becomes two… and soon he creates a simple rhythm:

CLAP — CLAP — PAUSE! 👏👏✨

Kiko hears the rhythm and wants to try. Then Mimi joins the game too! Together, the three friends learn to listen carefully, remember the pattern, copy one another, and keep the same rhythm.

Along the way, little learners can practice:

👏 Listening and copying
🎵 Simple rhythm and patterns
🧠 Memory and attention
🤝 Taking turns and playing together
💛 Friendship and teamwork
