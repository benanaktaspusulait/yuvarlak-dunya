## Action and Dialogue

**0.0–1.0s:** EXTREME CLOSE-UP on Kiko's face. Her eyes are already wide. Tiny blink. Small breathing. Background is soft blurred green.

**1.0–3.0s:** Kiko's mouth opens into a small "O". She whispers with wonder: "Oh! What is THAT?" Her eyes dart to the right side of the frame. Pigtails bounce once gently.

**3.0–5.0s:** Camera begins pulling back slowly. Kiko's full head and pigtails are now visible. The background starts to sharpen. Kiko leans slightly forward, curious.

**5.0–7.0s:** Camera continues pulling back. Kiko is now visible from waist up. Behind her and to the right, a GIANT STRAWBERRY comes into view — as tall as Kiko, bright red with green leaves, round and shiny, preschool-safe cartoon strawberry. It sits on the soft grass.

**7.0–8.5s:** Camera settles into medium-wide. Kiko and the giant strawberry are both clearly visible. Kiko's expression shifts from surprise to delight. She clasps her hands together.

**8.5–10.0s:** Kiko looks at the camera and says happily: "A strawberry!" She giggles softly. Hold final pose with both Kiko and strawberry visible.
