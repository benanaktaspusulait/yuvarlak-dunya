# Luca and the Footprints That Walk Ahead

## Two-Shot Production Package

This package replaces the earlier single-shot version with two separate clips:

| Clip | Duration | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Shot 1 | 5 seconds | Luca walks forward and discovers three footprints appearing ahead of him. |
| Shot 2 | 10 seconds | Luca tests the footprints by walking backwards, then speaks to the viewer. |
| **Final edit** | **15 seconds** | The two clips are joined at the exact matching frame with no transition. |

The completed story contains exactly **five footprints**: three in Shot 1 and two more in Shot 2.

## Required Frame Workflow

1. Create and approve `@image1`, the clean vertical opening frame for Shot 1.
2. Generate Shot 1 using `@image1`.
3. Export the clean final frame of Shot 1. This becomes the mandatory `@image2` start frame for Shot 2.
4. Prepare and approve `@image3`, the final anchor frame for Shot 2: Luca is still, amused, and all five footprints are visible.
5. Generate Shot 2 using `@image2` as its start frame and `@image3` as its final-frame reference.
6. Join the clips directly. Do not add a dissolve, crossfade, zoom, sound bridge or other transition. If the editor duplicates the shared boundary frame, remove only the duplicate frame.

## Canonical Decisions

- Character: approved Luca reference only.
- Setting: Little Forest — Discovery Path.
- Format: 1080×1920, 9:16 vertical, 24 fps.
- Style: calm Pompom Hills preschool animation.
- Audio: quiet natural ambience and soft effects; no music.
- Dialogue: English, spoken once in Shot 2 only.
- Continuity: Shot 2 must begin with the exact last visual state of Shot 1.
