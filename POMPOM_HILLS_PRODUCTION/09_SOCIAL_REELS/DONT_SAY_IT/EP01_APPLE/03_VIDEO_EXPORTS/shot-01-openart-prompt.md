## Action and Dialogue

**0.0–2.0s:** Both characters are still. Opa holds the apple at belly height. Arda looks at the apple with wide eyes. Small breathing movements only.

**2.0–4.0s:** Arda points at the apple and shakes his head playfully: "Don't say APPLE!" Arda's expression is firm but playful — like a game show host. Opa nods slowly, eyes wide behind glasses.

**4.0–6.0s:** Opa tilts his head 12 degrees to the right (thinking pose). He lifts the apple slightly with his wing. He looks at the apple, then at Arda.

**6.0–8.0s:** Opa holds up one wing (counting hint): "It's red…" Opa's expression is warm and trying-not-to-laugh. Arda crosses his arms, watching carefully.

**8.0–10.0s:** Opa makes a round shape with both wings: "It's round…" Opa's beak opens slightly as if he's about to say it. Arda leans forward, eyes narrow suspiciously.

**10.0–12.0s:** Opa pretends to take a bite of the apple (wing near beak): "You can eat—" Opa catches himself, eyes go wide behind glasses. Arda jumps slightly: "Opa!"

**12.0–14.0s:** Arda grabs the air in frustration (playful, not angry) and says loudly: "APPLE!" Both characters freeze for 0.5 seconds in surprise.

**14.0–15.0s:** Both characters look at the camera with warm smiles. Opa adjusts his glasses once. Arda gives a small wave. Hold final pose.
