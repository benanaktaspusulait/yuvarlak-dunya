## Story and Timing

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 00–04s | Luca walks carefully around the outside of the broad tree trunk. His shadow takes a visual shortcut by gliding across the visible trunk surface and arrives on the far side first. |
| 04–07s | Luca notices the shadow one step ahead and pauses in surprised curiosity. |
| 07–10s | He takes one gentle step toward it; the shadow slides one matching step ahead and stops. |
| 10–13s | Luca tries once more. The shadow again remains exactly one step ahead. |
| 13–15s | Luca stops, points softly and smiles; the shadow stops completely in a readable final pose. |

## Visual and Motion Direction

- Keep Luca fully visible inside the centre 60% safe area, occupying about 15–20% of frame.
- Preserve his approved mountain shirt, green shorts, striped socks, orange sneakers, freckles and rounded tousled hair.
- The shadow is always a single soft, grounded silhouette connected to the scene's light direction. It never gains eyes, a face, thickness or independent character anatomy.
- The “shortcut” reads as the flat shadow gliding visibly across the rounded trunk surface, not passing through a hole or creating a tunnel.
- Exactly two catch-up tests after the first shortcut.
- Camera performs one slow stable lateral settle; no chase camera.

## Audio

- Quiet Little Forest ambience with light leaves and soft Luca footsteps.
- Add one very soft dry “swish” as the shadow crosses the trunk.
- No dialogue, voices, narration, music, melody, song or chimes.
