## Action and Dialogue

**0.0–2.0s:** Both characters are still. Opa holds both fruits forward at chest height. Luca looks at the fruits. Small breathing movements.

**2.0–3.5s:** Opa looks at the camera with an excited expression and says: "Quick! Pick one!" His glasses shift slightly on his nose. Both fruits are prominently displayed.

**3.5–5.5s:** Opa holds the apple slightly forward (left wing): "Apple!" Then holds the banana slightly forward (right wing): "Banana!" His head turns between the two.

**5.5–7.5s:** Luca puts his hand on his chin (thinking pose). He looks at the apple, then at the banana, then back at the apple. His eyebrows furrow slightly. Small head tilt.

**7.5–9.0s:** Luca's face lights up. He points decisively at the banana and says: "Banana!" Big confident smile.

**9.0–10.5s:** Opa starts to hand the banana to Luca. As Opa extends his wing, the banana "slips" — it slides down and Opa catches it just before it falls. Opa's eyes go wide behind glasses. Luca jumps back slightly.

**10.5–12.0s:** Both characters freeze for 0.5 seconds, then burst into warm laughter. Opa adjusts his glasses. Luca waves at the camera. Hold final warm pose.
