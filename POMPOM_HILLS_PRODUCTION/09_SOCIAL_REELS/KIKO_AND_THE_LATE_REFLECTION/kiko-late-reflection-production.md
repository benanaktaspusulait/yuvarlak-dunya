## Story and Timing

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 00–03s | Kiko notices her clear reflection in the puddle and lifts one hand. |
| 03–06s | She gives one slow wave. The reflection remains completely still in its initial pose. |
| 06–09s | Kiko lowers her hand. Only then does the reflection perform the same slow wave and lower its hand. |
| 09–12s | Kiko looks surprised and waves once more. The reflection again waits until her real hand begins to lower. |
| 12–15s | The reflection completes the delayed wave. Kiko tilts her head and smiles in quiet amazement; the camera and water settle. |

## Visual and Motion Direction

- Kiko remains fully visible in the centre 60% safe area and occupies about 15–20% of the vertical frame.
- Preserve Kiko's exact approved face, brown pigtails with coral bands, coral long-sleeve shirt, white shorts, coral socks and rounded shoes.
- The reflection must remain a vertically mirrored Kiko in the exact puddle position. It may be delayed only in arm motion; it must never become a second physical Kiko.
- Exactly two real waves and exactly two delayed reflected waves.
- The puddle stays small, shallow, still and safely away from Kiko's feet.
- Camera is locked in a gentle slightly-above eye-level composition so Kiko and the reflection are readable together.

## Audio
