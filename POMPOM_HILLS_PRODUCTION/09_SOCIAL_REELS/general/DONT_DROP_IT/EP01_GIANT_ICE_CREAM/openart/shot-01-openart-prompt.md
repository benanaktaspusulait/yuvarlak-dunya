## Action and Audio Timeline

### 00.0–00.5 seconds — CLEAN HOLD

`@image1` is held exactly. Kiko stands still holding the giant ice cream. Calm pose. No wobble yet. This is the first-frame continuity hold.

### 00.5–02.0 seconds — THE SETUP

The top lavender scoop begins to wobble slightly. Kiko's eyes widen. She whispers:

**"Uh-oh…"**

Her body shifts slightly as she tries to balance the ice cream. The wobble increases.

### 02.0–04.0 seconds — THE DROP

The top lavender scoop slides off the stack. Kiko makes one clear grab attempt and misses. The scoop tumbles through the air.

### 04.0–06.5 seconds — OPA ENTERS AND REACHES

Opa waddles in from screen-left with quick small steps. He reaches with his wing-tips toward the falling scoop. The scoop bounces lightly off his wing-tip and flies upward.

### 06.5–08.0 seconds — SCOPE IN AIR / OPA IN FRAME

The lavender scoop is in the air above Opa, beginning its descent toward his head. Opa is now fully in frame, looking up toward the scoop. Kiko watches from screen-right with wide eyes and an open mouth.

Stable composition. This is the Shot 01 final frame — scoop above Opa, Opa in frame looking up, Kiko watching.
